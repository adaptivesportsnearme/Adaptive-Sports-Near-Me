About Us

Adaptive Sports Near Me is the idea of Alec Tranel and Karen Atkinson.



They saw the need for a reliable and up to date database for adaptive sports, helping people find the right sport.



They took their idea to RPIs RCOS program, and met with a group of students, who agreed this was a necessary service.



Working in close collaboration with Alec and Karen, a group of RPI students has developed the data collection tools, website, and data storage, bringing ASNM to life



While still in its early stages, we hope that ASNM can help people across the United States find sports programs that work for them, and support their needs
